Privacy policy

Version 2026-09-27. MySanaLife is a free wellness app. It has no advertising, sells nothing and never sells or rents your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile, meals, water, weight, activity, sleep and your family members' profiles are stored on your phone, in an encrypted database.
  • The app works fully without an account and without internet.
  • Data read from Health Connect or Apple Health stays on your phone unless you turn on sync.
  • Children's growth measurements stay on the phone unless the parent turns on sync.
  • Location, only if you choose it: for prayer-time sync and Ramadan meal times, the app uses either a city you pick from a list or, with your permission, the phone's approximate location (never the precise one, never in the background). It is kept on your phone only, used only to calculate prayer times, and never sent.

Health Connect

Only if you choose automatic or combined activity tracking, and only with your permission, the app reads these records from Health Connect: steps, exercise sessions, active calories burned and sleep sessions, for the last 7 days.

  • They are used only to show your activity and sleep in the app, to count towards your daily goals and to build your personal report.
  • The app only reads. It never writes to Health Connect or changes anything there.
  • These records are never sold, never shared with advertisers or data brokers, never used for advertising, and never used to decide on credit or insurance.
  • They leave your phone only if you create an account and turn on sync, and then only into your own protected records.
  • You can remove the permission at any time in Health Connect, or switch to manual tracking in settings.

If you create an account

  • We keep your email address and a protected password so you can sign in on another phone.
  • What you log is copied to our database (Supabase) so it is not lost if you change phones. Only you can read it.
  • We keep a record of the date and version of this policy you agreed to.

What is sent, and why

  • Barcodes you scan are looked up in our database, the USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. Only the barcode is sent, never who you are. A barcode we could not find is counted, without any personal data, so we know which products to add.
  • Questions to "Ask about your food" are answered on your phone. A question it could not answer is counted, without any personal data.
  • Emails (your sign-up code, password reset and a welcome message) are sent through Google's email service.
  • Notifications use a device token so we can send announcements, such as maintenance or a new version.
  • Crash reports, when enabled, contain technical details about the error and the phone model, not your health data.
  • Usage counts are anonymous totals (for example, how many people opened the app today). They contain no names and no health data, and no advertising or analytics company receives them.
  • Problem reports you send contain what you write, plus the app version, phone model and language.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off notifications, greetings, reminders, Health Connect or sync at any time in settings.
  • Download a copy of your data from settings.
  • Delete your account and everything stored with it from settings, or by writing to us. Deletion is permanent.
  • You may ask us to correct or explain the data we hold about you.

Age

You need to be at least 13 (16 in the European Union) to create your own account. Younger children can be added by a parent in the family section, where the parent manages their data.

Security

Data on your phone is encrypted, the connection to our servers is encrypted, and access to your records is limited to you by the database itself. If a breach ever affected your data, we would tell the authorities and you as the law requires (in Saudi Arabia, within 72 hours).

Changes

If this policy changes, you will see a message in the app's inbox before the change takes effect.
